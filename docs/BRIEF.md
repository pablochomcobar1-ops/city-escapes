# City Escapes — Project Brief (Stage 1)

_Concept project by Pablo Chomcobar. Not a real development. Last updated 7 Nov 2026._

## 1. What this is
A premium, dark-luxury website for a fictional Ahmedabad builder, **City Escapes**. It exists to showcase frontend craft: cinematic motion, a scroll story, and a real-time 3D walkthrough of a sample flat.

| Item | Decision |
|---|---|
| Type | Portfolio concept (no SEO, no paid tools) |
| Project | 2 towers · 24 floors · 3 & 4 BHK · SG Highway, Ahmedabad · under construction, sample flat ready · "Price on request" |
| Sample flat | **Residence 12A**: living + dining, kitchen, master bedroom, balcony with a city view (other rooms are closed doors marked "Coming soon") |
| Palette | Near-black base, warm stone / brass / linen accents |
| Primary CTA | "Book a site visit": a form with a demo success state ("This is a concept, so no request was sent") + WhatsApp click-to-chat |
| 3D | Full walkthrough built in code (React Three Fiber) with CC0 furniture (Poly Haven, Kenney) and CC0 HDRIs. Desktop: WASD + mouse-look. Mobile: tap hotspots to glide between rooms |
| Assets | Generated by the user in Gemini (stills) and Google Flow (video) from prompts I provide after Stage 2 |
| Kit | GSAP + ScrollTrigger, Lenis, motion/react, React Three Fiber + drei, custom grain/gradient shader, 1–2 React Bits effects (chosen in Stage 2) |
| Footer | "Concept project by Pablo Chomcobar. Not a real development." |

## 2. Positioning (product-marketing)
- **Core idea:** a calm retreat inside a busy city.
- **Tagline:** *Your escape, within the city.*
- **For** people buying a luxury 3/4 BHK in Ahmedabad who want quiet, light and space without leaving the city.
- **Unlike** typical builder sites (carousels, superlatives, "Enquire Now" everywhere), City Escapes **lets you walk through the home before you visit** and books a visit on your terms.

## 3. What visitors want and fear (customer-research + last30days)
| They fear | How the site answers |
|---|---|
| Possession delays: the most common buyer complaint, and much of the current buyer talk is about RERA delay rights | A "Construction progress" timeline with milestones and a stated possession date |
| Renders that don't match reality ("artistic impression") | The walkthrough plus an honest specifications sheet (materials and brands per room) |
| Quality issues after handover (seepage, finishes) | A "Built to last" materials section: structure, waterproofing, fittings |
| Sales pressure on staged site visits | "Visit on your terms": pick a weekday/weekend and a morning/evening slot; the copy promises no pushy calls |

| They want | Where |
|---|---|
| To see the space before driving out | `/tour` + home teaser |
| Light, views, quiet | Scroll story on home |
| A quick, low-commitment next step | 3-field booking + WhatsApp |

## 4. Competitor scan (competitor-profiling, quick, 7 Nov 2026, homepages only)
| Site | What they do | Gaps observed |
|---|---|---|
| Adani Realty | Render carousel, project search, many carousels, testimonials | Cluttered, repeated slides, generic copy; no tour or site-visit booking seen on the homepage |
| Shivalik Group | Static skyline hero, manifesto copy, stats, brand video | Stats show "0+" (counters not rendering), repeated blocks, no project visuals or next step |
| Goyal & Co | Project carousel, legacy film, values, city project lists | Duplicate nav, "#" links, no booking or tour seen |

**Takeaway:** all three sell the *company*. City Escapes sells *one home*, shown honestly, with one clear next step. That's the gap the concept fills.

## 5. Brand voice (brand-voice)
Quiet, confident, sensory. Short lines. No exclamation marks, no "world-class", no "luxury redefined".
- ✅ "Mornings, unhurried." · "The city stays outside." · "Light, from three sides."
- ❌ "Experience unparalleled luxury living!"

## 6. Site architecture (site-architecture)
```
/            Home
/tour        Residence 12A walkthrough (3D loads only here)
/about       The idea, the architect's intent, materials, progress
/contact     Book a site visit + WhatsApp + map
```
**Nav:** Logo · Residence · Tour · About · **Book a visit** (button). Mobile: full-screen menu.

**Home sections (draft order):**
1. Hero: cinematic video, word-by-word headline reveal
2. Intro statement: "Your escape, within the city."
3. Tower story: scroll-driven 3D or video of the towers, key facts (2 towers, 24 floors, SG Highway)
4. Light and space: pinned scroll story through interiors
5. Residences: 3 BHK / 4 BHK cards (area, highlights, "Price on request")
6. Step inside: walkthrough teaser → `/tour`
7. Built to last: materials and specs
8. Construction progress: timeline
9. Book a visit: CTA band → `/contact`
10. Footer: concept disclaimer, credit, socials

**About:** the idea · architecture and light · materials · progress timeline · CTA.
**Contact:** booking form (name, phone, preferred day and slot) · WhatsApp · location map (static, no API key) · demo success state.
**Tour:** loading screen with progress · controls hint · 4 zones with hotspots · room info cards · exit to "Book a visit".

## 7. Out of scope
SEO / OpenSEO, paid APIs, backend, accounts, payments.
